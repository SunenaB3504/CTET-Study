# How to Switch Between Paper I and Paper II

## Quick Answer
You can **change paper type anytime** using either:
1. **Header Icon** (Quick Access) - Click the Paper Type button in the top navigation bar
2. **Sidebar Button** (Full Menu) - Use "Change Paper Type" in the Settings section

---

## Method 1: Header Icon (Recommended - Fastest!)

### **📍 Location:**
Top navigation bar, right side (next to Home and Sun icons)

### **🎯 Look For:**
A button showing your current paper:
- **"Paper I"** or **"Paper II"**
- With a circular arrow icon (↻)
- Light blue background

### **📋 Steps:**
1. Look at the **top right** of the screen
2. Click the **Paper I** or **Paper II** button
3. Confirm when prompted
4. Select your new paper type
5. Done!

**Visual Example:**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Welcome to CTET Prep Pal, Sumi!  [↻ Paper I] 🏠 ☀│ ← Click here!
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### **Tooltip:**
Hover over the button to see:
> "Currently on Paper I (Classes 1-5). Click to change."

---

## Method 2: Sidebar Button (Alternative)

1. **Open the Sidebar**
   - On desktop: It's visible on the left side
   - On mobile: Click the menu icon

2. **Scroll to the Settings Section**
   - Look for the "Settings" heading near the bottom
   - You'll see it after the "Planning" section

3. **Click "Change Paper Type"**
   - Icon: ↻ (circular arrow)
   - Located under "Settings" section

4. **Confirm Your Choice**
   - A dialog will ask: "Are you sure you want to change your paper type?"
   - Click **OK** to proceed
   - Click **Cancel** to stay on current paper

5. **Select New Paper Type**
   - You'll be taken to the Paper Type Selection screen
   - Choose Paper I or Paper II
   - Click to select

6. **Continue Your Preparation**
   - You're now ready to study for the new paper type!

## What Happens When You Change?

### ✅ What's Preserved:
- Your saved preferences
- Your experience level selection
- Historical progress data (stored separately)

### 🔄 What's Reset:
- Current test session (if any)
- Subject selection returns to first subject
- View returns to Dashboard

### ⚠️ Important Notes:
- If you're in the middle of a mock test, **you'll lose your current test progress**
- The app will ask for confirmation before changing
- You can change back anytime using the same method

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: "I Selected Paper I by Mistake"
**Solution:** Click "Change Paper Type" → Choose Paper II

### Scenario 2: "I'm Preparing for Both Papers"
**Solution:** Use "Change Paper Type" to switch between them as needed
- Study Paper I content → Switch → Study Paper II content

### Scenario 3: "I Don't See Paper II Tests"
**Reason:** Paper II questions haven't been added yet
**What You'll See:** "No Paper II Tests Available" message
**Solution:** Continue with Paper I or wait for Paper II content

## Alternative Method (Advanced Users)

### Using Browser Console
If the sidebar method doesn't work, you can reset manually:

1. Open browser developer tools (F12)
2. Go to **Console** tab
3. Type: `localStorage.removeItem('ctet_user_preferences')`
4. Press Enter
5. Refresh the page (F5)
6. You'll see the Paper Type Selection screen again

**⚠️ Warning:** This clears ALL your preferences, not just paper type!

## FAQ

**Q: Where is the fastest way to change paper type?**
A: Click the **Paper I/Paper II button** in the top navigation bar (header). It's always visible and just one click away!

**Q: Will I lose my progress if I change paper type?**
A: No, your historical progress is saved separately. You'll only lose the current active session.

**Q: Can I switch multiple times?**
A: Yes! Switch as many times as needed using either the header button or sidebar.

**Q: Do I need to log in again?**
A: No, it's just a preference change, not a logout.

**Q: What if I want to prepare for both papers?**
A: You can switch between them anytime. However, the app shows content for only one paper at a time for a focused experience.

**Q: Where is the button on mobile?**
A: The header button shows the icon (↻) only on mobile. On desktop, it shows "Paper I" or "Paper II" text as well.

**Q: I don't see the paper type button in the header. Why?**
A: The button only appears after you've selected a paper type for the first time. If you haven't selected one yet, you'll see the Paper Type Selection screen first.
