NCFSE-2023 Curriculum Structure:

Aims of School Education: Develop rational thought, autonomy, health and well-being, democratic and economic participation, and cultural rootedness.

5+3+3+4 Stage Design:

Foundational Stage (ages 3–8): Play-based, focus on foundational literacy and numeracy, two languages.
Preparatory Stage (ages 8–11): Activity/discovery-based, two languages, mathematics, art, physical education, “The World Around Us.”
Middle Stage (ages 11–14): Three languages, mathematics, science, social science, art, physical education, vocational education.
Secondary Stage (ages 14–18): Multidisciplinary, choice-based, two languages, subjects from art, physical education, vocational, social science, science, mathematics, interdisciplinary areas.
Curricular Areas:

Languages (multilingualism, at least two native Indian languages)
Mathematics (emphasis on conceptual understanding, problem solving, computational thinking)
Science (inquiry-based, core concepts, scientific temper)
Social Science (integrated, thematic, rooted in Indian context)
Art Education (visual arts, music, dance, theatre)
Physical Education and Well-being (sports, yoga, health)
Vocational Education (work with life forms, machines/materials, human services)
Interdisciplinary Areas (environmental education, etc.)
Pedagogy: Experiential, inquiry-driven, discussion-based, project-based, arts/sports/activity-based, with reduced content load and focus on core essentials.

Assessment: Shift from rote memorization to competency-based, formative and summative, board exams to be easier and more flexible.

Values and Dispositions: Ethical, moral, democratic, epistemic values; positive work ethic, curiosity, pride and rootedness in India.

School Culture: Inclusive, nurturing, safe, supportive of teachers, community and family engagement.
