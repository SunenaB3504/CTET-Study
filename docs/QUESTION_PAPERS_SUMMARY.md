## Manual Extraction Guide

### For Paper II Questions (Target: 750 questions across 5 papers)

#### Step 1: Extract from PDFs
```bash
# From root directory
python extract_questions.py "SQP/Paper2/Qus-Aug-23.pdf"
python extract_set_i_answers.py "SQP/Paper2/Anz-Aug-23.pdf"
```

#### Step 2: Subject Classification for Q91-150
**Determine by examining question content:**
- **Science indicators:** physics, chemistry, biology, experiments, scientific method
- **Social Studies indicators:** history, geography, civics, economics, culture

#### Step 3: Topic ID Mapping

**For Science (SubjectName.SCIENCE):**
- `matter-nature` - States of matter, properties, changes
- `living-world` - Plants, animals, life processes, ecosystems
- `motion-measurement` - Force, motion, energy, simple machines
- `natural-resources` - Air, water, soil, conservation
- `science-pedagogy` - Teaching methods, experiments, inquiry-based learning

**For Social Studies (SubjectName.SOCIAL_STUDIES):**
- `history-culture` - Historical events, cultural heritage, movements
- `geography` - Maps, landforms, climate, resources
- `civics-polity` - Constitution, government, rights, democracy
- `economics` - Resources, occupations, development
- `social-studies-pedagogy` - Teaching methods, projects, field trips

**For Language I (SubjectName.LANG1):**
- `reading-comprehension` - Passage-based questions
- `grammar` - Parts of speech, tenses, sentence structure
- `vocabulary` - Synonyms, antonyms, idioms, word meanings
- `writing-skills` - Composition, paragraph writing
- `language-pedagogy` - Teaching methods, assessment

**For CDP (SubjectName.CDP):**
- `child-development` - Stages, theories, domains
- `learning-theories` - Piaget, Vygotsky, Kohlberg, Gardner
- `assessment` - Formative, summative, continuous evaluation
- `inclusive-education` - Special needs, diversity, accessibility

#### Step 4: NCF 2005 Explanation Format
Every explanation should reference NCF 2005 principles:
```typescript
explanation: "This [subject] question examines [concept]. Understanding [principle] is crucial for Classes 6-8 [subject] curriculum. NCF 2005 emphasizes [relevant pedagogy aspect] and developing [relevant skill] in learners."
```
